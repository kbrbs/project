About This Virtual Tour

Welcome to the Virtual Festival Tour of the Minasa Festival!

This interactive experience lets you explore our cultural heritage through:

  • Historical insights and stories
  • Traditional recipes and cooking methods
  • Cultural performances and dances
  • Agricultural practices of our farmers

Each booth is designed to bring you closer to the heart and soul of Bustos. Click on any booth to explore, watch videos, view photos, and take quizzes!

Enjoy your journey through our festival!
